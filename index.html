FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Do you deliver outside Ahmedabad? +

Yes! While we offer same-day delivery within Ahmedabad, we also ship across Gujarat and India via courier. Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days for out-of-city orders.

Are all your products original and safe? +

Absolutely. We are authorized dealers for all major brands. Every product meets BIS safety standards and is suitable for the recommended age group.

What is your return policy? +

We offer a 7-day no-questions-asked return policy for unused products in original packaging. Simply bring the item to our store or arrange a pickup.

Can I place orders via WhatsApp? +

Yes! You can browse our catalog and place orders directly on WhatsApp at +91 98765 43210. Our team will confirm availability and arrange delivery.

Do you offer bulk discounts for schools/events? +

Yes, we offer special pricing for bulk orders of 10+ units. Contact us for custom quotes for school events, birthday parties, and corporate gifting.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards, net banking, cash on delivery, and wallet payments. EMI options available for orders above ₹5,000.

Ready to Make a Child's Day?

Visit our Naranpura store or order online. Same day delivery available across Ahmedabad.
